Pod Cascade

Nine people, one shared record.

AjoFam was designed and built by Pod Cascade. Each member owns a page and a discipline — which is the same idea as the product: everyone knows where they stand.

The brand idea

Everyone knows where they stand.

The name joins Ajo and Fam — a trusted financial family. Everything we designed had to serve one of five pillars, or it did not go in.

01

Clarity

The most important information is visible without searching for it.

02

Trust

Clear records support the trust that already exists between people.

03

Community

Ajo is people helping people. The product should sound like a member, not a bank.

04

Accountability

Facts, without public shaming. A missed payment is information, not a verdict.

05

Simplicity

If someone understands the notebook, they understand AjoFam.

The brand sheet

What we designed to

These are the decisions the whole site is held against. If a page breaks one of them, the page is wrong — not the sheet.

Colour

  • Ajo Green #176B4DPrimary. Buttons, links, the loop.
  • Deep Charcoal #18221DText and interface.
  • Warm Cream #F8F6EFBackground. Paper, not screen.
  • Fresh Mint #DCEFE5Supporting and positive states.
  • Contribution Gold #D5A83AKey moments — next to collect.
  • Alert Red #C94C4CMissed and overdue. Used sparingly.

Type

DM Sans

Display and headings. Warm, geometric, confident.

Inter

Body, navigation, records and buttons. Built for small sizes and numbers.

Status language

Paid Pending Missed Next to collect Collecting

Five states, five words people already use. No jargon, and no word that blames anyone.

What we ruled out

  • Looking like a bank, or like a spreadsheet
  • Fintech jargon — no wallets, no escrow, no yield
  • Shaming anyone for a missed payment
  • Hiding key information behind extra clicks
  • Decoration that does not help someone read the record
Ajo should be easy to understand, even when the money is not yours alone.

AjoFam is not trying to change how people save together. It is making the contributions, the rotation and the rules easier for everyone to see.
