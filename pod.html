The loop, made visible

Ajo runs in a loop — contribute, wait your turn, collect, then start again. AjoFam shows exactly where your group is in that loop, so nobody has to ask.

Missed payments, without the shame

When someone misses a contribution, AjoFam records it as a fact — not a public callout. Everyone can see what was due, what the group agreed happens next, and what's needed to catch up.
