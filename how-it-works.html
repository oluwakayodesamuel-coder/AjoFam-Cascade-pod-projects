HOW AJO WORKS

Everyone knows what happens next.

AjoFam makes traditional Ajo and Esusu contributions easier to follow by making the rotation, payments and next turn visible to everyone in the group.

THE SIMPLE IDEA

No guessing. No asking the group chat. Your contribution and your position in the rotation are always clear.

THE PROCESS

From joining the group to completing the cycle.

01

Create or join an Ajo group

Start a contribution group with people you trust, or join an existing group with a clear contribution agreement.

02

Agree on the contribution

Everyone knows how much to contribute, how often payments are made and how many members are in the rotation.

03

Make your contribution

Each member contributes according to the agreed schedule. Payment records make it clear who has paid and who is still pending.

04

Follow the rotation

The group can see the order of members and exactly who is next to receive the pooled contribution.

05

Receive your turn

When your turn arrives, the group knows it. The rotation stays visible instead of depending on memory or scattered messages.

06

Complete the cycle

Everyone continues contributing until every member has received their turn and the agreed cycle is complete.

WHAT EVERYONE CAN SEE

The information that keeps the group accountable.

01

Payment status

See which members have paid and which contributions are still pending.

02

Next in line

The rotation order makes it clear who is next to receive the contribution.

03

Missed contribution

A missed or overdue contribution can be identified instead of being lost inside a busy group chat.

WHEN SOMEONE MISSES A PAYMENT

The group sees the situation, not just a missing message.

AjoFam keeps payment status visible so members can identify a pending or missed contribution early and handle it according to the group's agreed rules.

The goal isn't to shame anyone. It's to make the information clear enough for the group to respond fairly.
