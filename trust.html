Trust & verification

The record is only worth what it can be checked against.

AjoFam asks your group to rely on a page. That is a fair thing to be careful about — so here is exactly how a payment becomes a record, who can change it, and what we do not do.

The boundary

AjoFam never touches your money

This is the most important thing on this page. AjoFam is a record, not a bank and not a wallet. Contributions move exactly the way your group already moves them.

What AjoFam does not do

  • Hold, receive or move your contributions
  • Take a cut, a fee or interest from the pot
  • Decide who collects, or change your rotation
  • Lend money, or offer any credit product
  • Report anyone to anyone

What AjoFam does

  • Write down what the group agreed, once
  • Record each contribution as paid, pending or missed
  • Show the same record to every member at the same time
  • Remind people before a deadline, not after
  • Keep the history so nobody has to remember it

Verification

How a payment becomes a record

Four steps, and a member can check their own entry at every one of them.

01

The member pays

Cash to the coordinator, a bank transfer or mobile money — whichever methods the group agreed when it was set up.

02

The coordinator marks it

The contribution is marked paid on the group page, on the same day, with the date and the method it came in by.

03

The whole group sees it

Every member is looking at the same page. A record that only one person can see is the problem we started with.

04

The member checks their own line

This is the step that does the work. If your name does not show paid for the week, say so straight away — not three cycles later.

The honest version

A transfer screenshot on your phone is not proof to the group — it is proof to you. It becomes the group’s record when it appears on the group page, which is why checking your own line after you pay matters more than keeping the screenshot.

Who can do what

One person writes. Everybody reads.

Ajo already works this way — one coordinator collects and keeps the book. AjoFam keeps that arrangement, and removes the part where only they can see it.

What each role in an AjoFam group can see and change
Action Member Coordinator
See the full rotation Yes Yes
See every member’s status Yes Yes
See the group’s rules Yes Yes
Mark a contribution paid No Yes
Change the rotation order No By group agreement
Raise a correction Yes Yes
Delete history No No

A correction never erases what was there. The original entry stays, with the correction shown beside it and dated — so the fix is as visible as the mistake.

If the group disagrees

What to do when the record and a member don’t match

01

Raise it the same week

Differences are easy to settle while the week is fresh and whoever handed over the money still remembers it.

02

Bring what you have

A transfer reference, a date, the person who witnessed the cash. The record is corrected on evidence, not on volume.

03

The group decides, in the open

The correction is applied on the same page everyone reads, so no private side-agreement can contradict it later.

Your information

What we keep, and why

Only what the record needs

A name your group recognises, a contact for reminders, your seat and your contributions. We do not ask for bank credentials, a BVN, or anything a savings notebook would never have held.

Visible to your group only

Your record is shared with the members of your own group, because that is the entire point. It is not sold, not published, and not shown to other groups.

Missed payments stay internal

A missed contribution is information for your group and nothing more. It is not a credit score, it does not follow you, and it is not reported anywhere outside the group.

The history stays yours

When a cycle closes, the record of it remains readable to the group. Closing a cycle should not mean losing the proof that it happened.
