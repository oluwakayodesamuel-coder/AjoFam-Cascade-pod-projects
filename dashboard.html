Question four

What happens if someone misses

Written once, at the start, and the same for everyone for the length of the cycle. Nobody has to ask, and nobody gets to improvise.

  1. 1

    ₦15,000 every Friday, cash or transfer to the coordinator.

    Ten members, ten weeks, one full cycle.

  2. 2

    The order is fixed.

    It was set before week 1 and does not change on request. Only the missed-payment rule below can move anyone.

  3. 3

    A missed payment doesn’t disappear.

    It is added to the following Friday. Miss once and you owe ₦30,000 next time.

  4. 4

    Two misses in a row moves your seat to the back.

    You drop to last position and whoever was behind you moves up. You still owe everything missed — this changes when you collect, not what you owe.

  5. 5

    Collecting your turn doesn’t end your payments.

    You keep contributing every week until the cycle finishes.

  6. 6

    The cycle closes after week 10.

    Once everyone has collected, the group meets to confirm every account is clear before starting again.

On the missed payment above

Amaka Chukwu’s week 6 contribution has not come in, and she also missed week 5. Under rule 4, one more miss moves her seat to position 10. This is shown as information, so the group can reach her before that happens — not as a public callout.
