Get started · about three minutes

Start your Ajo group.

Set your contribution, choose how often you collect, and bring your members in. AjoFam keeps the record from the first payment, so there is never an argument with the book.

When you submit, AjoFam creates your group’s page. The rotation, the record and the rules all live there, and update as your members join and pay.

The default rules

How your group will run

Every AjoFam group starts on these five rules, so members know what to expect before the first contribution. Your group can agree its own variations once the page exists.

  1. 1

    Everyone pays the agreed amount, on the agreed schedule, in the agreed order.

    The amount and the order stay fixed once the group starts — whether or not it is a member’s turn to collect. Everyone keeps paying until every seat has collected once.

  2. 2

    The pot goes to whoever’s seat it is, once everyone has paid.

    You confirm every contribution before the pot is handed over. If one person has not paid, the collector waits — the pot does not go out short.

  3. 3

    A missed payment carries over to the next round.

    If a member does not pay by the deadline it does not disappear. It is added to what they owe the following round, and they keep their seat.

  4. 4

    Miss two rounds in a row and the seat moves to the back.

    Their turn moves behind whoever was last in line. They still owe everything missed — this changes when they collect, not what they owe.

  5. 5

    Once a seat has collected, it cannot collect again this round.

    Every seat gets exactly one payout per round. When all seats have collected, the group either repeats the same order or agrees a new one — by discussion, not by memory.
