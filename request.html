How this group runs

The same five rules the group agreed on paper, written out so nobody has to remember them right.

1

Everyone pays ₦5,000, every Friday, in the order above.

The amount doesn't change and the order doesn't change, whether it's your turn to collect or not. You keep paying every week until all ten seats have collected once.

2

The pot goes to whoever's seat it is, once everyone has paid.

The coordinator confirms all ten payments before handing over the pot. If even one person hasn't paid, the collector waits — the pot doesn't go out short.

3

Missing a payment means you owe double the next week.

If you don't pay by Thursday night, you still owe that ₦5,000. It carries over — you pay ₦10,000 the following week, on top of staying in your seat.

4

Miss two weeks in a row and your seat moves to the back.

Your turn to collect moves behind whoever was last in line. You still owe everything you missed — this changes when you collect, not what you owe.

5

Once you've collected, you can't collect again this round.

Every seat gets exactly one payout per round of ten weeks. After week 10, the group either starts a new round in the same order or agrees a new one — by discussion, not by memory.
