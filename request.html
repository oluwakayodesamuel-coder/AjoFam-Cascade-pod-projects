How your group will run

These are the default AjoFam rules every group runs on, so members know what to expect before the first contribution is made.

1

Everyone pays the agreed amount, on the agreed schedule, in the rotation order.

The amount and the order stay fixed once the group starts, whether or not it's a member's turn to collect. Everyone keeps paying until every seat has collected once.

2

The pot goes to whoever's seat it is, once everyone has paid.

You confirm every payment before the pot is handed over. If even one person hasn't paid, the collector waits — the pot doesn't go out short.

3

Missing a payment carries over to the next round.

If a member doesn't pay by the deadline, it doesn't disappear — it's added to what they owe the following round, on top of staying in their seat.

4

Miss two rounds in a row and the seat moves to the back.

The member's turn to collect moves behind whoever was last in line. They still owe everything missed — this changes when they collect, not what's owed.

5

Once a seat has collected, it can't collect again this round.

Every seat gets exactly one payout per round. Once every seat has collected, the group either starts a new round in the same order or agrees a new one — by discussion, not by memory.
