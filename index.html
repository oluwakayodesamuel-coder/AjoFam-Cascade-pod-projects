What the notebook can't do

These are the exact moments coordinators run into every cycle — not hypothetical pain points, the real ones.

No proof of payment

A member says they paid. The coordinator's notebook says otherwise. There's no shared record to settle it.

Lost or damaged records

One notebook, one coordinator. If it's misplaced, water-damaged, or the coordinator is unavailable, the group's history goes with it.

Unclear missed-payment rules

Members don't know what happens when someone misses a turn, which turns confusion into disputes.

One shared record, always current

  • See the rotation order and exactly who collects next.
  • Check every member's contribution status for the current cycle.
  • Understand missed-payment rules in plain language, agreed by the group.
  • Get reminders before a contribution is due — not after it's missed.
