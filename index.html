The problem

What the notebook can’t do

These are the moments coordinators hit every cycle — not hypothetical pain points, the real ones.

01

No proof of payment

A member says they paid. The notebook says otherwise. There is no shared record to settle it, so it becomes one person’s word against another’s.

02

One notebook, one keeper

If it is misplaced, water-damaged, or the coordinator is travelling, the group’s entire history goes with it.

03

Unwritten missed-payment rules

Members don’t know what happens when someone misses a turn, so confusion turns into a dispute nobody planned for.

Read the full problem we researched

The solution

One shared record, always current

AjoFam doesn’t change how ajo works. It replaces unreliable memory and scattered records with a single page the whole group can open.

  • See the rotation order and exactly who collects next.
  • Check every member’s contribution for the current cycle.
  • Read the missed-payment rules in plain language, agreed by the group.
  • Get a reminder before a contribution is due — not after it is missed.
  • Look back at any member’s full history without asking anyone.

What we stand on

Facts, not fault-finding

A missed payment is information the group needs, not a public shaming. AjoFam records what happened and what the group agreed happens next — nothing more.
