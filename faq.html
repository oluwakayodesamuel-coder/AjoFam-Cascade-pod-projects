Guide & FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Written in the words your group already uses — paid, pending, missed, next. If your question is not here, ask us and it gets added.

Start here: the four words

AjoFam runs on four states, and nothing else. Learn these and you can read any group page.

Paid

The contribution came in and the coordinator recorded it.

Pending

Due, but the deadline has not passed yet. Nothing is wrong.

Missed

The deadline passed without the contribution. It carries over.

Next to collect

This member receives the pot at the end of the current round.

Question not answered?

Ask the team

Paying in

I already collected my turn. Do I still have to pay every week?

Yes. Your turn being over does not mean you are finished — you pay until the cycle ends, the same as everyone else. The rotation decides when you receive money, not when you stop putting it in.

What happens if I miss a payment?

It does not vanish. It is added to what you owe the following round. Miss once and you pay double next time, and you keep your seat.

What if I miss twice in a row?

Your seat moves to the back of the rotation. Whoever was behind you moves up. You still owe everything you missed — this changes when you collect, not what you owe.

Can I pay early, or pay for several weeks at once?

Yes, if your coordinator agrees. Paying ahead is recorded against the weeks it covers, so your line shows paid for each one rather than a single lump nobody can account for later.

Collecting your turn

Can I swap my turn with someone else?

Only if you both agree and tell the coordinator before the cycle starts. Once the order is set and the first payout has gone out, it is locked for everyone — that is what makes it fair.

I need money urgently. Can I collect before my turn?

No. The whole point of ajo is that everyone waits their turn. Asking to jump the line is asking someone else to wait longer for money they were already counting on.

What if I am collecting this week but I also owe a missed payment?

You still owe it. Collecting does not cancel a debt to the pot — it is settled alongside your collection, so the total stays correct for the members still waiting.

What if not everyone has paid when my turn comes?

The pot does not go out short. Your coordinator confirms every contribution first. If someone is outstanding, the group applies its own rule rather than quietly handing over less.

The record

How do I know my payment was actually recorded?

Check your line on the group page after you pay. If it shows paid for the week, it is recorded. A transfer screenshot on your phone is proof to you — it becomes the group’s record when it appears on the page.

The page and my memory disagree. Whose word counts?

The page — it is updated the same day money moves, in front of whoever is around, so there is no gap for anyone to misremember later. If you believe it is wrong, raise it with your coordinator that week.

Can a record be changed after the fact?

A correction can be made, but it never erases what was there. The original entry stays with the correction shown beside it and dated, so the fix is as visible as the mistake.

Who can see my contribution history?

The members of your own group, and nobody else. That is the point — a record only one person can see is the problem AjoFam exists to solve. It is not sold, published, or shown to other groups.

Running a group

Can someone new join partway through a cycle?

Not as a collector — that would mean someone already in line waits even longer. New members join at the start of the next cycle, added to a fresh order.

What happens when the cycle finishes?

The cycle closes. The group meets, confirms every account is settled, and decides together whether to run again in the same order or draw a new one.

Does the coordinator follow the same rules?

Yes. They contribute every round like everyone else and hold a seat in the rotation. A missed payment from the coordinator follows the exact same catch-up rule.

Does AjoFam hold our money?

No. AjoFam is a record, not a bank or a wallet. Contributions move exactly the way your group already moves them — cash, bank transfer or mobile money — and no fee is taken from the pot.

What does AjoFam cost?

Nothing to your pot. AjoFam does not take a cut, charge interest, or lend money. If that ever changes, it will be stated plainly here before it applies to any group.

Words we use

Ajo
A rotational savings group. A set number of people contribute a fixed amount on a fixed schedule, and take turns receiving the pooled total. Also called esusu or adashe.
Cycle
One full round of the rotation, from the first contribution until every seat has collected once.
Seat
A member’s position in the rotation. Seat 3 of 10 means you collect third.
The pot
The pooled total for one round — the contribution amount multiplied by the number of members.
Coordinator
The member who collects contributions and marks the record. Every group already has one; AjoFam just gives them a better book.
Carry-over
A missed contribution added onto what you owe the following round.
