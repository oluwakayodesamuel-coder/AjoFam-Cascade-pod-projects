How this group runs

Written once, the same for everyone, for the length of this cycle.

  1. 1

    ₦15,000, every Friday, cash or bank transfer to Oluwakayode. Ten members, ten weeks, one full cycle.

  2. 2

    The order is fixed. It was set before week 1 and doesn't change on request — only the missed-payment rule below can move someone.

  3. 3

    A missed payment doesn't disappear. It's added to the following Friday. Miss once, you owe ₦30,000 next time.

  4. 4

    Two misses in a row moves you to the back. You drop to the last position in the rotation; whoever was after you moves up.

  5. 5

    Collecting your payout doesn't end your payments. You keep contributing every week until the whole cycle finishes.

  6. 6

    The cycle closes after week 10. Once everyone's collected, the group meets to confirm every account is clear before starting again.

Questions people actually ask

If yours isn't here, ask Oluwakayode directly and it'll get added.

I already collected my payout. Do I still have to pay every week?+

Yes. Your turn being over doesn't mean you're done — you pay until week 10, same as everyone else. The rotation only decides when you receive money, not when you stop putting it in.

What happens if I miss a Friday?+

It doesn't vanish. It gets added to whatever you owe the following Friday. Miss once, you pay double next time.

What if I miss twice in a row?+

You lose your place in line. Whoever's turn was right after yours moves up, and you go to the back — position 10.

Can I swap my collection turn with someone else?+

Only if you both agree and tell Oluwakayode before the cycle starts. Once the order is set and week 1 has paid out, it's locked for everyone.

I need my payout early — can I ask for it before my turn?+

No. The whole point of ajo is that everyone waits their turn. Asking to jump the line is asking someone else to wait longer for money they were already counting on.

How do I know my payment actually reached Oluwakayode and got recorded?+

Check this page after you pay. If your name shows a green "PAID" stamp for the week, it's recorded. A transfer screenshot on its own isn't proof until it shows up here.

What if Oluwakayode misses a week?+

Same rule as everyone. He pays into the pot every Friday like the rest of us, and he's on the rotation too — a missed payment from him follows the exact same catch-up rule.

The book — or this page — and my memory disagree. Whose word counts?+

This page. It's updated the same day money moves, in front of whoever's around, so there's no gap for anyone to misremember later.

What happens when we reach week 10 and everyone's collected?+

The cycle closes. We meet, confirm every account is settled, and decide together whether to start a new cycle with the same order or shuffle it.

Can someone new join partway through a cycle?+

Not as a collector — that would mean someone already in line waits even longer for their turn. New members join at the start of the next cycle, added to a fresh order.

I'm collecting this week but I also owe last week's missed payment — what happens to that?+

You still owe it. Collecting your payout doesn't cancel a debt to the pot — it's settled alongside your collection, so the total stays correct for the members still waiting their turn.
