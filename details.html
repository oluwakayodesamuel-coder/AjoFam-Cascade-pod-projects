What week 4 means

A missed payment is a fact, not a verdict.

Amaka missed week 4. The record says so plainly, and it also says what the group agreed would happen — and what has happened since.

What happened

The rule the group agreed

A missed contribution carries over and is added to the following week. It does not reduce what is owed, and one miss does not move anyone’s seat.

If something looks wrong

Say so, and the group settles it together.

Records are entered by a person, and people make mistakes. If a contribution you made is not showing, raise it with your coordinator — corrections are made on the same page, in front of everyone, so the fix is as visible as the error was.
